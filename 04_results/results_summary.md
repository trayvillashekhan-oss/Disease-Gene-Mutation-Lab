Sequence Comparison

The MAFFT alignment of the wild-type and documented mutant proteins showed a single amino-acid difference at position 188. Serine in the wild type was replaced by phenylalanine in the documented mutant. No insertion, deletion, or downstream frameshift was observed.

In comparison, the artificial c.563del mutation produced a much greater change in the predicted protein because deleting one nucleotide shifted the reading frame.


Conclusion

The results demonstrate that different types of DNA mutations can have different effects on a protein. The documented c.563C>T substitution produced a missense mutation affecting one amino acid, while the artificial c.563del mutation caused a frameshift that altered many downstream amino acids and introduced premature stop codons.
